# AeroFlap Assets

**Art direction:** Sunlit Paper Skies — a high-key, tactile editorial illustration with soft-ivory paper clouds, sky-blue depth, dark-ink outlines, richly verdant gate pillars, and a vermilion folded aircraft. The world should feel like a compact aviation logbook rather than a generic mobile-game scene.
