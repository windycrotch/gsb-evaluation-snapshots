Playhead coherence scenarios

Watch the progress fill and the time text. They must always point at the same moment and never carry state from a previous source.

  1. Switch source: click Source B mid-playback. The fill and time text snap to 0:00 immediately; no old position or first frame remains while the new source loads.
  2. Scrub and commit: drag the progress bar and release on a new position. The fill/time jump to the target on release and stay there even while the video briefly buffers — they never bounce back to the old value first.
  3. Pause / resume: pause, wait, then resume. The playhead freezes exactly where it was and continues from that point without stepping backwards.
  4. Failure retry: click Broken source. The player retries up to three times (two seconds apart) while the playhead stays in a defined state, then stops instead of retrying forever. Click Recover to load a working source again.
