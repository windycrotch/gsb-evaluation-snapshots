# Code of Conduct

We want Bone 2D to be a friendly place for everyone who animates, codes, translates or just asks a question.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Keep feedback specific and useful: what happened, what you expected, a file if you can.
- Respect other people's time: search before you ask, and say thank you when you get help.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep doing so.
To report a problem, open an issue or write to the maintainer through the contact on their GitHub profile.
