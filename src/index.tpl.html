Borders & Crowns

Turn-based grand strategy on the real world map.

How to play

  • New here? Press Tutorial above for a short guided practice game.
  • Click one of your provinces, then a highlighted neighbour, to move or attack.
  • Right-click a distant province to march there by the shortest route, turn after turn.
  • Gather all armies in one province, or give realm-wide orders, with one click.
  • Recruit soldiers and develop provinces with gold.
  • Open a foreign province to declare war, make peace or seek an alliance.
  • Orders are carried out when you press End turn.
  • Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.
